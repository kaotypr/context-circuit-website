<HomeHero>

Context Circuit is a living documentation system for a product or project. It gives coding agents accepted context before they change code, coordinates work across repositories, and brings durable knowledge back in line when the work is complete.

</HomeHero>

<HomeSplit>

## The circuit

A request can say exactly what to change and still leave out why the current behavior exists. Context Circuit closes that gap on every change, and brings what the change taught back into the project's knowledge.

[Read the full product model](/docs/understand)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Living knowledge">Architecture, domain rules, decisions, and vocabulary are written down once and read before the next change.</Step>
  <Step title="An approved outcome">The intended outcome is written in plain language. Contradictions and missing decisions become questions for a person, who approves it.</Step>
  <Step title="Plans from real code">Plans come from reading the code, once the outcome is approved and you ask for them.</Step>
  <Step title="Code changes">The coding agent implements the plan in its own worktree and commits there. Pushing and merging wait for you.</Step>
  <Step title="Knowledge reconciled">When a change alters a durable rule, completion updates the knowledge so the next session does not rediscover it.</Step>
</Steps>

</HomeSplit>

## A workspace above your repositories

A workspace holds the understanding and records a team shares, including a team of one. Repositories hold the implementation.

<Features>
  <Feature icon="knowledge" title="Project knowledge" href="/docs/concepts/product-knowledge">Accepted architecture, conventions, decisions, and terminology, cataloged so an agent finds what bears on a change.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="records" title="Intents and plans" href="/docs/concepts/intents">The outcome you approved and the plans written from code, kept as records anyone on the team can read.</Feature>
  <Feature icon="repositories" title="Repositories" href="/docs/concepts/relationships">One outcome can coordinate several repositories. Local bindings say where each checkout lives, without storing machine paths in shared files.</Feature>
</Features>

</HomeCentered>

## Two products

They are released independently and can share a version number without sharing a release identity. The [changelog](/changelog) lists every release of both.

<Products>
  <Product track="template" title="Workspace template">The shared project folder: instructions, skills, knowledge, intents, and plans.</Product>
  <Product track="cli" title="Context Circuit CLI">The native tool that writes structured records, checks the workspace, and prepares working copies.</Product>
</Products>

</HomeCentered>

<HomeCallToAction>

It does not prove an agent understood the product, and it does not prevent every mistake. It makes assumptions inspectable and keeps the consequential choices with you.
