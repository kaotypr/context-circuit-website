The workspace template and the CLI are released independently. The [changelog](/changelog) lists every release of both, and filters by product or version without coupling the two version numbers. These guides describe the v2 model at the evidence baseline named on each page. Minor and patch releases are recorded in each changelog, which covers 2.0.0–2.5.0, stable releases only. Release candidates such as 2.5.0-rc.1 have no notes of their own.
