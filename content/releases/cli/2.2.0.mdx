CLI 2.2.0 creates plans as folders with `plan.md` as the entry. A plan may include
supporting files assigned as shared or repository-specific reading. The CLI
reports missing, unsafe, unlinked, or unassigned details.

Plans may be created without an intent when a person directly requests a grounded
plan for an already specified outcome. Use `record order --plan ID` to select an
exact run. Existing single-file plans remain readable without migration.
